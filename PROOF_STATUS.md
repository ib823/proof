## Caveat: `Parameter` declarations are axioms in all but name
In Coq a `Parameter` is logically equivalent to an `Axiom`. The active build
contains **30 `Parameter` declarations**. The propositional ones
(`..._sound`, `..._correct`, etc.) are the documented Trusted Hardware/Crypto
Primitive interface — unproven by design because they model external physical
measurements or opaque crypto functions, not Coq-provable facts (e.g.
`domains/PhysicalSecurity.v` EDA/X-ray/PUF/FIPS-140-3 entries; `argon2id_hash`
in `domains/VerifiedIdentity.v`, RFC 9106). The headline "Axioms (active) = 0"
is therefore true only because these are spelled `Parameter`; they are tracked
here separately and are NOT gated to 0.
